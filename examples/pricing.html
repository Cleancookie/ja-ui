Billing questions

Can I change plan later?

Any time, in both directions. Changes are prorated to the day, and downgrading never deletes anything — the extra seats simply stop being billable.

What counts as a seat?

One person who can publish a theme. Everybody who only reads the docs or uses an app you built is free, and always will be.

Do you offer invoicing?

On the Enterprise plan, yes — annual invoicing with purchase order numbers and net-30 terms. Ask for it in the sales form.

Is there a free tier for open source?

The library is MIT licensed, so an open source project already has everything. Email us and we will move you onto Team for nothing.

Still deciding?

The library itself is MIT licensed. Use it free forever and pay us only when you want a human to answer the phone.
